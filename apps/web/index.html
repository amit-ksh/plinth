How it works

From connected to fixed in three steps

  1. 01

    Connect or import

    Sign in with Shopify, or bring two spreadsheets. Plinth reads products, costs and discounts once, and audits them in your browser.

  2. 02

    Every stack, checked

    A graph of which discounts reach which products, and which pairs allow each other. Plinth searches it for every group that can apply together.

  3. 03

    The smallest fix

    One setting to change, what else it breaks, and a preview. Make it from Plinth, or roll it back from history.

Under the hood

A stack is a clique in a graph

Discounts are nodes. Two discounts that can apply together share an edge, with the rule that allows it. A stack is possible only when every pair in it connects, so Plinth grows fully connected groups on Relate, Neo4j’s embedded graph database, in your browser and on disk.
